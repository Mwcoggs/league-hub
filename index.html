How it's built

Player projections come from Sleeper's weekly feed, rescored with this league's rules (6-point passing TDs, full PPR, custom kicker and defense scoring). Each week the model starts the highest-projected legal lineup from active roster players; taxi and IR spots are excluded and players on bye score zero.

Power score blends the rest-of-season rating with actual points versus average; actual results count 10% after week 1 and grow to 50% by week 9. Win chances assume each team's weekly score varies by about 30 points around its projection. Playoff seeding uses wins, then points for, and the bracket runs 4 vs 5 and 3 vs 6 in week 15, with the winners facing seeds 1 and 2 in week 16 and the final in week 17.

The loser's parlay board is on the honor system: everyone adds a leg before Sunday 1:00 PM Eastern, the previous week's low scorer places it, and whoever placed it records the result.

Data refreshes every morning from Sleeper. Spreads are the projected margin rounded to the nearest half point and are frozen once posted. Later-week projections are rougher and don't know about future injuries or trades.
